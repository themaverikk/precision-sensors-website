River monitoring

Flow Discharge Radar System

A velocity radar and a level radar working together tell you how much water a river is carrying. They also give an early flood warning, because speed rises before the level does.

  • Reports discharge in m³/s
  • Separate units or one combined unit
  • Early warning in a flood
1

Measure the speed

A 24 GHz radar beams at the flowing surface and reads the surface speed.

2

Measure the level

A 60 GHz radar measures the distance down to the water surface.

3

Work out the flow

With the river cross-section, speed and level give the volume of water per second.
